Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of design work do you specialize in?

I specialize in branding, UI/UX design, and digital illustrations. I also create visual content for social media, websites, and marketing materials.

Which tools do you use for your projects?

I mainly use Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and Figma. Depending on the project, I also use Canva, After Effects, and Procreate.

How do you approach a new design project?

I start by understanding the client's goals and target audience. Then I sketch concepts, create mood boards, and design iterations while gathering feedback until the final design is polished.

Can you handle both digital and print design?

Absolutely. I design for both digital platforms--like websites, apps, and social media-- and print media such as brochures, business cards, and posters.

Do you offer custom illustrations?

Yes! I create unique digital illustrations, icons, and graphics tailored to each project to make designs stand out.

How can I get in touch for a project?

You can reach me via the Contact section on this portfolio, or directly through email and social media links provided below. I usually respond within 24 hours.
